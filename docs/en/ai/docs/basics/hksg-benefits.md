> **Applicable regions**: HK / SG · **Nature**: a time-limited higher-tier trial card (a service usage benefit—not a cash rebate, not a securities reward)
> Specific eligibility conditions, tiers, and validity periods are governed by **the in-product Benefit Center display and the corresponding campaign terms**.

## What Is a Benefit Card

A benefit card is a **time-limited card for experiencing a higher plan tier**: once activated, for its validity period you enjoy **that tier's feature benefits and usage quota** (e.g. a Pro trial card → Pro-tier features and quota).

Three core principles:

| Principle | Description |
|------|------|
| **Does not change your subscription** | A benefit card creates no subscription and attaches no payment conditions, nor does it affect renewals or charges on your existing subscription. It falls back automatically at expiry and **will not auto-renew** (benefit card ≠ free trial) |
| **Changes the plan benefits you enjoy** | During the validity period, feature permissions follow the **higher of** your current subscription tier and the card tier—Skill count limits, knowledge base capacity, third-party MCP, etc. are all judged at the higher tier |
| **Usage stacks** | Paid users: remaining weekly quota + the card tier's weekly quota; Starter users: quota allocated directly at the card tier |

## How to Get One and How to Activate It

**How to get one**: benefit cards are not publicly sold and cannot be purchased. They are issued through the following channels—

- **Campaign milestones**: issued automatically when you participate in a platform campaign and meet its conditions
- **Task rewards**: issued after completing tasks in the Task Center
- **Targeted platform grants**: operations issues cards to specific users (you may be notified via email, community channels, or App notifications)

**Issued ≠ active**: after issuance, the card first goes into the **Benefit Center** (status: unused). You must **actively click "Use Now"** to activate it:

```
Receive a benefit card → open the Usage page and see the "You have 1 unused benefit card" prompt
  → click through to the Benefit Center → click "Use Now" → the benefit activates and takes effect immediately
```

- The Benefit Center is an in-App feature; Web users should activate in the App
- **Validity is counted in days** (e.g. 7 / 30 / 90 days), starting from activation
- A card you never activate **expires automatically** after the unused-expiry window (180 days by default; the terms prevail)

## Usage Rules

| # | Rule | Description |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | **Card tier must be ≥ your current tier** | A card below your current subscription tier cannot be used (clicking "Use Now" will show a prompt); a same-tier card works and purely stacks usage |
| 2 | **Only one card active at a time** | While a card is active, a new card cannot be used—wait for the current card to expire (queued auto-activation is not supported) |
| 3 | **Your subscription is unaffected during the period** | The original subscription renews normally; if you **upgrade your subscription** during the period, the card stays active, usage keeps stacking, and features are judged at the higher of the two tiers |
| 4 | **Unsubscribing during the period doesn't affect the card either** | The card stays active until it expires; after expiry, you fall back to Starter |
| 5 | **Fallback at expiry** | Features fall back to your original plan tier; the card's remaining usage is **reclaimed outright**; content created over the limit (e.g. Skills) is not deleted, but no more can be added |
| 6 | **Non-redeemable, non-transferable** | Benefit cards are service usage benefits; the platform reserves the right of interpretation for campaigns (the terms prevail) |

## Where to Check

- **Usage page**: a benefit card status card at the top—trial tier + remaining days + the stacked weekly available quota; an entry prompt is shown when you have unused cards
- **Plan page**: shows the current trial tier, remaining days, and your original subscription status
- **Benefit Center**: view all benefit cards and their status (unused / in use / used / expired)

## FAQ Quick Reference

| Question | Answer |
|------|------|
| I got a Pro trial card—can I use Pro features? | **Yes**. During the validity period your feature permissions rise to the Pro tier (Skill limits, knowledge base capacity, etc. are judged at Pro) |
| I already subscribe to Pro—is a Pro card useful? | Yes. Activating a same-tier card **stacks usage** (remaining weekly quota + the card's weekly quota); your subscription is unchanged |
| I subscribe to Premium and received a Pro card? | It cannot be used—a card below your current plan tier does not take effect |
| Will a benefit card auto-renew or create a subscription? | No. Benefit cards are pure giveaways with no subscription and no payment conditions |
| What if there's unused quota on the card at expiry? | It is reclaimed outright and your original plan quota resumes; features fall back at the same time |
| What if I never use a card I received? | It expires automatically after the unused-expiry window (180 days by default) |
| How do I get a benefit card? | Watch platform campaigns and the Task Center, or wait for a targeted platform grant; purchase is not supported |
